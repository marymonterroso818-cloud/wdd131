Workplace Safety Training Hub

Workplace Planning Document

Site Name

I chose "Website Safety Training Hub" because a hub represents a central point of connection. In this case, it serves as a central location for training resources and training tracking.

Site Purpose

This website provides a centralized safety training library where users can easily access essential workplace safety courses and guidelines. It also includes an interactive training tracker to help individuals and managers monitor course completion and stay up to date. on required certifications.

Scenarios

  • Which workplace safety training topics are required for my specific job role?
  • How can I track and monitor my team's safety training completions?

Color Schema

  • Navy Blue (#233D4D): Header, headings, and footer.
  • Safety Orange (#D97706): Buttons, accents, and highlights.
  • Light Gray (#F8FAFC): Main page background

Typography

  • Merriweather: Main Headings and section heading.
  • Roboto: Paragraphs, lists, and navigation.
